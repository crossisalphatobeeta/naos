Why we're called NaosLearn

Every lesson should show a child where it can take them.

Naos is one of the hottest and most luminous stars visible to the naked eye. Sailors once steered by stars like it. We chose the name because good teaching should do the same: give a child a direction.

Indian families around the world invest a great deal in their children's education, but most tuition never goes beyond the textbook. Children learn to solve the problem, but rarely see why it matters or where it could lead.

So we built NaosLearn around professionals from the world's leading industries. They teach the curriculum, share their real work, and help children grow into confident, curious learners who are ready for a global future.
